Challenge

Landlords on Picogrid wanted chargers but not the paperwork. The platform had no energy licence and no plans to get one.

Solution

Embed’s components sit inside the Picogrid app. Solvane is the supplier of record, so landlords simply pick a price and collect.

Results

A feature that became a business

One in three buildings switched charging on in the first year, and €9 million has been paid out.

With Solvane, our landlords went from asking about chargers to earning from them.

Jonas Weber, Founder
