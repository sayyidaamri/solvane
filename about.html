Solvane is a technology company focused on making clean power easier to run, share, and afford.

We build programmable energy infrastructure, rethinking from first principles how electricity should be bought, stored, and used, so that any business can start and grow.

Thousands of businesses build on Solvane, from single-site founders to continent-wide fleets. They use Solvane as a core service to charge vehicles, launch tariffs, and keep buildings warm. Together they managed 19 terawatt-hours last year.

When power becomes more flexible, everyone gains. More sun and wind can be used, less is wasted, and the cost of a busy evening is shared more fairly.

Our own growth depends on the businesses that build with us, so we invest back into the platform every day. We make improvements that add up, maintain reliable APIs, and develop new tools for markets and safety that keep more lights on.

We also believe that good grids come from good communities. Alongside our tools we publish ideas and fund long-term work on storage, materials, and repair, with the hope that they stay useful for a very long time.
